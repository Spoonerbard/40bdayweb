Ignorance is no defence

01 The Rules

The official guidance for contestants and guests. Participation is voluntary; enjoyment is compulsory only in spirit.

01

Taking part

Every task is optional. Take on as many or as few as you like, including none at all. There is no minimum or maximum number of people who can take part in a task. The more tasks you complete, the more points you can win. This is for fun, not to embarrass anyone.

02

Playing fair

You may share information, work with other players, and get help from anyone you wish. Collaboration is allowed; choose the approach that makes the tasks enjoyable for you.

03

Safety and access

Break no laws and do not endanger yourself or others. If you have a safety or access concern, contact the Taskmaster. The Taskmaster will make all final decisions.

04

Time and submissions

Complete and submit tasks by 23:59 on 20th March 2027. Submit each completed task to the Taskmaster as a video, unless that task specifies another proof format. Upload your submission using the form below; a Google account is required.

Upload a task submission using the Google Form ↗

If you cannot upload through the form, contact the Taskmaster directly to submit your video or photo.

05

The final word

The Taskmaster will judge the tasks. Their decision is final, arbitrary, and legally unchallengeable.

Event details: Date, arrival time, venue, travel advice and dress code remain on the guest briefing.
