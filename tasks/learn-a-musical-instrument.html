Official task briefing · VT task 06

Learn a Musical Instrument

Learn a tune on a small acoustic instrument, then prove it is recognisable.

Your task

Learn to play a recognisable melody on an acoustic musical instrument shorter than your forearm. You must perform live for the Taskmaster (or submit an uncut 30-second video). The song must be instantly recognisable to the Taskmaster without prompt. Best musicianship wins.

The Taskmaster's decision is final
