# Tasks

## rules
- All tasks must be completed and submitted by 23:59 on 20th March 2027.

- Break no laws.

- Do not endanger yourself or others. 

- The Taskmaster’s decision is final, arbitrary, and legally unchallengeable.

## Prize Tasks
"Bring the greatest birthday gift for the Taskmaster that cost £5 or less. You have 60 seconds to pitch your gift. Best gift wins."

## VT Tasks
### remote initials
"Physically transport the Taskmaster’s initials (SJF) as far away from Chertsey, England as possible. You must personally accompany the object, or write the initials yourself at the final destination. The initials must be visibly marked onto a solid, non-electronic object. Furthest straight-line displacement from Chertsey wins."


### Optical Illusions
"Stage the most convincing high-stakes disaster using forced perspective and household objects. No digital manipulation, filters, or actual damage permitted. Your submission must be a single, unedited photograph taken from a single vantage point. Most catastrophic illusion wins."

### Portrait 
"Create the most dramatic portrait of the Taskmaster eating a sandwich. You must create the piece by hand using physical media—strictly no AI generation or basic photo printouts. Bonus points if an actual edible sandwich ingredient forms part of the artwork."

### Umbrella
Do the most inventive and surprising thing with a standard umbrella. The umbrella must remain central to the act. Photographic or video proof required (maximum 60 seconds). Most delightfully unexpected use wins.

### ADventuring in style
Assemble a distinct adventurer’s outfit using only garments and gear currently in your possession, then embark on an epic quest. Submit a video report of no more than 60 seconds (or precisely 5 photos) documenting your trial. Most heroic narrative and attire wins.


### Learn a Musical Instrument
Learn to play a recognisable melody on an acoustic musical instrument shorter than your forearm. You must perform live for the Taskmaster (or submit an uncut 30-second video). The song must be instantly recognisable to the Taskmaster without prompt. Best musicianship wins.


### The Loophole
"Present the cleverest exploit of an established rule, law, or social convention. You have 90 seconds to demonstrate your loophole to the Taskmaster. Most ingenious subversion wins."


### Get down with the party tunes
"Submit the best party-starting track that the Taskmaster has never heard before. If the Taskmaster already recognises the song, you receive zero points. The most danceable, unknown track wins."


### Loo roll the mother of invention
"Execute the most astonishing feat of engineering or art using empty cardboard toilet roll tubes. You may not use sheets of paper, nor may you purchase empty tubes in bulk online—they must be salvaged naturally. Most breathtaking creation wins."
